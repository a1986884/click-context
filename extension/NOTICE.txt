Paragraph translations are provided by Cloudflare Workers AI and labeled as machine translation.
Dictionary lookups read limited excerpts from the requested public Collins
English–Chinese section hosted by Youdao, with links to the requested source.
Collins dictionary content remains copyright HarperCollins Publishers.
No full dictionary corpus or API credentials are bundled.
The original demonstration articles/glossary are identified as CC0-1.0 examples;
they are not used as fallback Collins definitions in the extension.

Chromium dictionary HTML is parsed with native DOMParser in an offscreen page.
No third-party HTML parser is bundled in the Chromium extension.
